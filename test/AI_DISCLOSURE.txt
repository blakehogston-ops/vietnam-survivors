Steam AI content disclosure (v3, FINAL, approved by Blake Oct 21, 2026, 11:50 PM ET)

Pre-generated content: Some art (squad portraits, store icons) was created with an AI image generator and reviewed by the developer. In-game code and procedural art were written with AI assistance. One licensed background music track may have been AI-generated by its creator. Most character voice lines were generated with an AI text-to-speech service (ElevenLabs) using licensed voices, then edited and processed by the team; the mission briefing is recorded by the developer. Some store-page text was drafted with AI assistance and edited by the developer. Some background music was generated with AI (ElevenLabs Music) and selected and edited by the team.

Live-generated content: None. All AI-generated content was created during development; the game does not generate content with AI while running.
