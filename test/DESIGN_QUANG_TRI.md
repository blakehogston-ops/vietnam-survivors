# Quang Tri design direction (Blake, Oct 7, 2026, 7:58 PM ET)
Status: received, NOT started. This supersedes FEEDBACK_PLAN.md wherever they conflict.
Core: fifteen Marines, a signal, a pad, and a bird that leaves. The swarm is the weather. The score is who got on.
Hard rules: no parry, no dodge roll, no skill tree; never respawn the platoon; never reward shooting civilians.

Milestone 0 ("Stop" test): fresh save, Doc only, no god mode. Forced first level-up = Magnet. Find smoke, confirm, hold pad, card names who got on. No debug line anywhere. If it fails, stop; do not open another map. After the first tester run, change ONE thing (lost the smoke / left the pad / misread the bunker).

Full text of Blake's spec (sections: Seed, Blockers, Fifteen and the birds, Combat and the base, One call, Screen and sheet, Sound) is in the room transcript of Oct 7, 2026 7:58 PM ET; key points:
- Seed: picks call, net lie, file entries, live tunnel mouth, trapped dike. Fixed: the 15, smoke confirm, pad, bird. Seed shown on card; replayable; fresh launch never repeats last seed.
- Blockers: strip debug strings; death/win cards never say "DEBUG killed by"; win card never shows an old death. Line under timer: NO SIGNAL (commander drops 3:00) -> VIOLET SMOKE — 40m + arrow -> HOLD FOR LZ — 10:00; no signal by 9:00 drops the mirror. Firebase point: HOLD F TO CALL ENGINEERS + ground ring. Doc only open record on fresh save; others open when he comes home. Gunny, bosses, signal get text; other calls announce once then icon. Death card = one plain fact. Win card = who got on, who was loaded, optional call done; kills small; Hogston dedication + crew-chief line; seed. Heartbeat stays.
- Fifteen: 3 Hueys x 5 insert then leave; dead stay dead; no replacement call; count under timer only goes down. Skids touch 3 times: insert, one medevac, last bird. Door gunner never lands. medevac rules (patches living, loads critical/dead-if-camp, loaded leave count, no shooting, needs living corpsman, leaves ~10 s). Dead corpsman = no Triage/medevac; dead radioman = bird won't hold; extract needs one other Marine on the ramp. Bodies stay; abandoned men named on card. Pad = marked circle telegraphed at 8:00; off-pad hold doesn't count; bird leaves on a clock. Color confirm one tap; smoke shapes red banded / yellow solid / green two fingers / violet spiral; wrong confirm or Charlie's smoke = wrong clearing (cost is the sprint). Two radio tokens (radioman or crate only): mark, smoke, fire mission or medevac; handset = walk, no fire, short stick. Sky magazine 4 jets shared by Arc Light, Skyhawk, Ranch Hand, medevac; 0 = "Winchester". Net lies once a run (authenticate or strike walks onto the wire). Ranch Hand wilts cover and slows everyone incl. Doc. Noise discipline once at start.
- Combat/base: files of 6-10 off-map, path the file, max 8; entries on dike/treeline/riverbank/road. Rifle file walks until fired on then crawls; kill point man = scatter; sappers hit claymores/berm; RPG pairs target tank/firebase/Huey; commander leads a file, kill him = stall. Grass/treelines block sight not movement. Tunnel mouths visible mounds with lids, one live underground file, collapse with grenade/claymore/Doc standing on it. No pits inside built base; pits outside flash before biting; pit-immunity item; two hand flares reveal pits/mouths/pad. Readable multi-layer walls; Marines fight from bunkers (bunker dies first); Doc can shelter. Armor column once (M48, flame tank/Sheridan, last Patton) up the road.
- One optional call per defense (seeded, skippable, big XP + ribbon, no added men, doesn't pause clock): Bubba's lost squad, downed bird, reversed claymores, ammo crate, fake extract smoke. Two Medal of Honor calls from REAL citations (cut-off squad; corpsman under fire): card shows name, service, unit, date, short line quoted from the official citation; ribbon "in honor of", not titled Medal of Honor; never invent the deed. One village event (clinic/search/civilians on road), shooting them gives nothing. One dead-Marine camp (ammo or heal crate; holding it makes Dustoff take bodies).
- Screen: short item line + long text behind; short tutorial (handset stops your gun; one other Marine on the ramp). Category caps; capped category only shows owned options; distinct icons; listed combos. Flamethrower much longer, effective, clears grass. Capped fading blood, gore toggle stays, no body-part clouds. Smoke = short colored column. Yellow ring under Doc only. Men 32 px; Huey + portraits 48; draw once, cache, stamp; top-left light, one outline. First sheet: Doc, one VC, one paddy, violet smoke, shown in a fight first. Redo Huey as a UH-1 (rotor disc, skids, tail boom, wash circle). Records open only after Doc comes home; hometown line only if something specific happened.
- Sound: real licensed files for Huey rotor, M16, M60, mortar, distant bomb, flamethrower, short radio squelch; footsteps mud/boards/water/Huey deck. Free-for-commercial-Steam or bought packs; Legal checks license before shipping; no pack name + license line = not used; no film rips. Keep the pentatonic score and rotor bed. Cap concurrent gunshots; rifles quieter than bird/bomb/Gunny; Winchester ducks music to rotor. One quiet second on the smoke drop (hit-stop, duck, canister hit).

Conflicts with the current build to resolve: Company Reinforcement perk (adds 20 men) and patrol/squad reinforcement calls violate "no replacement"; Medevac perk overlaps the single Dustoff; character unlock plan becomes "Doc first, others open when he comes home"; the fifteen replaces the current squad/patrol/company system.
