LAST CHOPPER OUT: RIDE-IN ORDERS (for historical / wording review)

No named speaker. These five lines play as captions over the CH-46 cabin loop
(cutaway #0). They are five of the original eight launch-briefing lines, kept
word for word; the three dropped lines were the speaker's introduction and two
personal remarks. No recorded voice and no TTS.

1. Up here in First Corps we hold the northernmost provinces of South Vietnam. Khe Sanh. Quang Tri. Hue. Da Nang. Every hill, every road, every bridge matters.
2. Charlie knows this ground, and the North Vietnamese regulars come down the trail in strength. You'll face tunnels, punji pits, ambushes, mortars... and worse.
3. Our mission is simple to say and hard to do: hold the line, look after the man beside you, and give the people of this country a chance to live free.
4. You'll have a fire team on your flank, air and artillery on the radio, and a CH-46 waiting to take you in. Use all of it. Keep your head down and your eyes open.
5. Good luck, Marine. Make every round count. Semper Fi.
