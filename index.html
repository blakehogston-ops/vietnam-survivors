VIETNAM SURVIVORS

I Corps · 1968 · Hold the line against the VC and NVA
Move: WASD / Arrow keys (touch: drag anywhere)
Weapons fire automatically. Collect XP gems, level up, pick upgrades.
1 / 2 / 3 pick an upgrade  ·  P / Esc pause  ·  M mute
Support weapons (take the KC-135 Aerial Refueling upgrade for faster air support): 81mm mortar, 105mm howitzer, PBR river gunboat, strafing runs (A-4E Skyhawk, then F-4B Phantom with SUU-16 pod and A-6A Intruder; the old A-1H Skyraider drops in as an alternate), armor support (M48A3 Patton, M67A2 Zippo, M50A1 Ontos), Marine Fire Team insertion (a UH-1E drops 4 Marines, growing to an 8-man squad; a CH-46 Sea Knight flies the big ones), B-52 Arc Light, UC-123 Ranch Hand, and (unlocked later) a UH-1 Huey with door gunners.
Characters: pick one of 9 service records (they level up between runs and unlock). Space / ability button fires your special. R reroll and B banish on the level-up screen (2 each per run).
Firebase: find the LZ radio, hold for a few seconds and engineers build a base: regen, ammo, armor, turrets. Defend it from waves and sappers. Max-level pairs evolve: M60 + Ammo Pouch = AC-47 Spooky, Arc Light + KC-135 = Box Mission. Bosses drop supply crates.
Extraction mission (~12 min): the Gunny talks you through it on the radio. The 3:00 and 6:00 bosses drop a signal (colored smoke, flare, signal mirror) and a spare shows up at 9:00 if you have none. At 10:00 the LZ opens: press G (or tap the SIGNAL button), confirm your color, then hold the LZ for about 75 seconds while the Huey arrives (30 s) and waits (45 s). Stand in its ring for 2 seconds and get on the bird to win. If you die or it lifts off empty, the mission fails.
Survive as long as you can. Watch for ambushes, tunnel hatches, punji pits, B-40 rocket troopers (they hunt your tank and Hueys) and, deep in the run, NVA armor and the NVA commander!
