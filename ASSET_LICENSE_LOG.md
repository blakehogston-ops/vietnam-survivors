## Draft: Steam AI content disclosure (for Blake's approval; relayed approval not confirmed by Blake directly)
> **Pre-generated content:** The game's art and sprites are drawn at runtime by procedural code that was written with AI assistance (no AI image-generator output is included); tutorial images are screenshots of that art and reviewed by the developer. One background music track, licensed from a third party via Pixabay, may have been AI-generated by its creator. *[Delete this sentence if combat_4 is swapped for "Rock Oco".]* Some store-page text was drafted with AI assistance and edited by the developer. The only voice in the game is a briefing recorded by the developer. No AI or text-to-speech voices are used.
> **Live-generated content:** None. The game does not generate content with AI while it is running.
> **AI tools used in development (not shipped):** An AI source-separation tool (Demucs) was used only to check licensed music tracks for hidden vocals. None of its output ships in the game. *(Steam only requires this note if it's useful context. Source: Sound Helper, music/research/demucs_run.log.)*
